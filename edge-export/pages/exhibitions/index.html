BURGEONING

22 August - 20 September 2026 · Gallery Fractal, Shanghai

Group exhibition · Curated by Liu Li · PATCH × Gallery Fractal

Wen Shuyu's works in BURGEONING examine how memes multiply through repetition, misreading and algorithmic attention. A Minesweeper game is invaded by internet catchphrases; a navigation frame keeps pursuing the Moon with deadpan concentration; hair, glass, silicone and motors turn familiar forms into protective or deceptive signs. Exhibited works: Shuyu's Minesweeper (video game, 6 ED + 1 AP); Fluffy Desire (synthetic-fibre wig and motor); Leaders' Portrait Swimming Caps (silicone); Soft Weapon Series 001 (wig and glass, variable dimensions); Destination: Moon (mixed media and new-media moving image, 4 ED + 1 AP).
