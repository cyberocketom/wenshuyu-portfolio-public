2022 · Participatory installation

RED or DIED

Scratch cards, printed web pages, megaphone, mixed media

About the work

A megaphone calls to visitors in the manner of a market stall: “Come and play the scratch cards—you could be the next millionaire.” Participants scratch the cards to reveal pages associated with different censored terms.
