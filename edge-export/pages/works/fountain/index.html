Fountain

Printed tracing paper, stone · Variable dimensions

About the work

A found vintage slide of St Petersburg's Samson Fountain is compared with Google Maps views of the same site. Neither archive nor algorithm can complete the promised verification. Printed onto translucent paper and wrapped around an ordinary stone like a gift, the image shows how objects acquire authority through packaging, circulation and belief.
