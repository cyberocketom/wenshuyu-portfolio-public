Blind Spot of Desire

Slide projection

About the work

Two visual displacements share one projection: a crucifix appears on a political square, while two bald heads can be misread as intimate body parts. Sacred, political and erotic meanings depend on placement and projection; humour exposes how readily an image becomes evidence for what a viewer already desires to see.
