Intervention

Details+

Printed text, public-space intervention, live machine translation

About

1998—

Wen Shuyu works across installation, moving image, performance, photography, painting, sound and publishing. Her practice examines how repetition, misreading and translation turn constructed meanings into credible realities.

For her, art can function like cyber-witchcraft: a space where otherwise unrelated things occasionally connect.
