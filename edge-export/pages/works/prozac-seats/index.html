2022 · Interactive installation

Prozac Seat

Photograph, wooden seating structure, photoresistor, fan

About the work

A photograph records a cross-shaped pattern found on the ground while the artist was riding a camel in the Sahara. The form is remade as a seat for tired legs or minds; when a viewer directs light onto the photoresistor in the floor element, a fan begins to run.
