2021 · Intervention

Translation Failure

Printed text, public-space intervention, live machine translation

2021 · Shenyang, Liaoning, China

About the work

The introduction from the official website of an artists’ association was printed and carried through Shenyang. It was placed in a stone lion’s mouth and beside a crossing sign on a road without a zebra crossing, then reread through a phone’s AI scan-translation function.
